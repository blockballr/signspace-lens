# SignSpace — CLAD Week 4 Demo Voiceover
Target length: ~90 seconds. Each line is one narration beat with its timestamp
window. Speak at a relaxed pace; pause briefly between beats. Square-bracket notes
are what should be on screen — do not read them aloud.

---

[0:00–0:10 — SPECS 27 preview, hand at rest, HELLO begins signing]

What if you could speak — and a hand in the air signed it back in American Sign
Language? This is SignSpace.

[0:10–0:25 — hand plays HELLO; Logger shows "gloss: HELLO*"]

SignSpace turns English into ASL, live. Watch: it signs "hello" as a whole word —
that's what the star means in the gloss line at the bottom.

[0:25–0:45 — say "THANK YOU" — delegate fires it — hand signs, Logger shows
"heard: THANK YOU" and "gloss: THANK YOU*"]

It listens to speech, or typed text. "Thank you" is in its vocabulary, so it signs
the whole word — not letter by letter.

[0:45–1:00 — say "WATER" or any non-vocabulary word — Logger shows fingerspelled
letters; hand fingerspells]

Anything outside its vocabulary, it fingerspells — every letter, one at a time — so
it never fails to say something.

[1:00–1:15 — HUD still: b15-restart-mobile.png on screen]

The interface — your heard text, the current sign, and the ASL gloss — floats beside
you. This is the panel in our two-D preview; the desktop stereo preview has a known
Snap regression that hides screen-space layers, but the panel renders here in 2D, and
on the device.

[1:15–1:30 — screen: GitHub repo + CLAD_PROMPT_LOG.md]

And this entire Lens was built with CLAD — prompt, build, verify, repeat. Every round
is logged in the prompt log, from the first hand rig to the final fix.

[1:30–1:40 — back to the hand, wide shot]

SignSpace. Speak, and be understood in sign.

---

## Recording notes

- Total spoken word count is ~150; at a normal pace that lands between 80–95 seconds.
- If a beat runs long, cut the 0:45 fingerspell demo to one word and trim the
  1:15 repo segment to "Every round is logged — the repo and prompt log are linked
  below."
- Record with the Logger visible: the heard/gloss lines are the product's readouts.
- Phrases to fire in order: HELLO (auto on start), THANK YOU, WATER (fingerspell).
  The delegate drives these via MCP when you say them.
